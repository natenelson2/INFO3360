# What you can see

Welcome to hockey operations staff access. This short note tells you what your job can see on day one. We will never ask for your password in a message like this. If something looks closed, that is intentional — do not try to get around it.

## If you are an assistant scout

- You can see: Scouting notes and evaluation text for the players you help full scouts with. You may look at those notes, add new ones, and update them. You can also see game schedule, opponent, and score or result context so your notes line up with real games. You may use simple player labels (name, position, jersey) only to tag those notes.
- You cannot see: Health records, injury records, sports-science measures (such as training load or body measures), or short health labels such as injured or not cleared. You also cannot see separate free-text notes on a player card that are not the scouting evaluation itself. You cannot delete scouting notes.
- On shared scouting or game screens: Health or injury marks will not appear here either. Even when you open a normal scouting or game screen, you still will not see health or injury labels on that screen.

## If you are a video analyst

- You can see: Scouting note text about player tendencies and opponent strengths or weaknesses for the games you are breaking down — look only; you do not write or change those notes. You can also see game schedule, opponent, period and score context, and game markers used to find and label video. You may use simple player labels (name, position, jersey) only to tag clips and reviews.
- You cannot see: Health records, injury records, sports-science measures, or short health labels such as injured or not cleared. You also cannot see separate free-text notes on a player card beyond those simple labels.
- On shared scouting or game screens: Health or injury marks will not appear here either. You will not see everything on every screen — only the scouting and game facts your job needs.

## If you already work here

### Coaches

- You can see: Game schedule and game context you need for practices and games.
- You cannot see: Full scouting note bodies, health or injury records, sports-science measures, or health marks on shared scouting or player screens.

### Scouts

- You can see: Scouting notes for the players on your assignments (look, add, and update; not delete), plus schedule and opponent context for that work.
- You cannot see: Health or injury records, sports-science measures, or health marks on shared scouting or player screens.

### Medical and sports-science staff

- You can see: Health and sports-science information for the people in your care, including care-related health marks you already use. You may look at that information, add to it, and update it for care work.
- You cannot see: Scouting note bodies, or game and video operations information. Your job is care data, not game or video ops.

## If you are not sure

Ask your hockey operations lead (or the person who assigned your role). They can confirm what your job should see. Do not share passwords, and do not look for a way around a closed door.
